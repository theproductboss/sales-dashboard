# Your brand assets

Drop your files here, or just drag them into the Claude chat and Claude will put them in the right place.

- **logo/** — your logo (a PNG with a transparent background is best)
- **products/** — 3–10 clean photos of your best sellers
- **past-posts/** — 3–6 screenshots of posts or graphics you love from your own account
- **inspiration/** — optional: posts from other brands whose look you like (used for style only, never copied)
- **fonts/** — optional: brand font files you own (.ttf, .otf, .woff)
- **layouts/** — Claude saves your favorite designs here so it can reuse them
