# Competitor Research: Primally Pure
*October 21, 2026. Sources: each brand's own website and product catalog (checked today), plus retailer listings, review blogs and press. I could not see anyone's Instagram posts directly, because Instagram blocks automated viewing.*

## The big takeaway
Everyone in clean body care launched their holiday products in early-to-mid October. The tallow brands sell **winter skin + holiday scent**, and the mainstream brands sell **scent + gift sets + discounts**. Nobody combines **a non-toxic home** (candles, room spray, hand soap) **with tallow body care**. Primally Pure is the only one with both, and your best-performing reel was a *scent ritual*. Lean into "a non-toxic, cozy holiday home + body" in the Early window, before Black Friday noise.

## Brand snapshots

**Toups & Co Organics**: direct competitor (tallow skincare + clean makeup)
- **Angle:** "100% organic", tallow balms, a "Never List". Celebrating 10 years. Give $20, Get $20 referral.
- **Price:** $6 lip balm → $32 balms → $98 holiday set (median ~$86 across sets).
- **Holiday 2026:** *Mistletoe* tallow balm (fir, orange, nutmeg, $32), *Merry & Bright Set* ($98, "the holiday set for 'everything showers'"), *Candy Cane Lip Love* ($6), new *Warm Vanilla Body Butter* ($52). All published Oct 1–19.
- **Does well:** cheap, impulse-buy stocking stuffers + a "Non Toxic Starter Set" ($64) for first-timers.

**FATCO**: direct competitor (tallow body care, irreverent voice)
- **Angle:** "the leading producer of beef tallow skin care". Playful, cheeky names ("Stank Stop" deodorant, "Fat Stick", "Fat is Your Friend" merch).
- **Price:** $14 deodorant → $52 Winter Essentials Gift Set → $68 Travel Kit (median $28).
- **Holiday:** "Winter Essentials" Gift Set ($52) pitched on dry, irritated winter skin. Strong pregnancy/mama-to-be gift sets.
- **Does well:** humor and a clear problem → product ("winter skin is dry + irritated → here's the kit").

**Native**: mainstream "clean" deodorant competitor
- **Angle:** "Clean. Simple. Effective." 150,000+ 5-star reviews. Scent quiz, "Scent Stacking Set".
- **Price:** ~$13 median; bundles $38–57.
- **Holiday 2026:** Early Bird Sale ending 10/25, free gifts at $45/$65, 20% off bundles, 25% off subscriptions. Seasonal scents (Candy Cane, Gumdrop, Winter Cashmere).
- **Does well:** scent discovery (quiz, stacking) and massive review proof. **Discounts early and often**, which trains customers to wait for a sale (price is the #1 complaint in retailer reviews).

**Nécessaire**: dream brand / style crush (premium body care)
- **Angle:** "Treat Your Body Like Your Face®", "Hand Care Is The New Face Care", "less but better", "Find Your Signature Fragrance".
- **Price:** $12 → $200 (median $45). Gift sets $50–150.
- **Holiday:** gift-ready packaging (black metal gift tin, gift box + gift note), *The Hand Wash + Hand Lotion Gift Set* with a tray ($150), *The Body Ritual Kit* trial sizes ($50).
- **Does well:** elevated, editorial framing. Turns everyday products (hand wash) into a giftable *ritual*, sold by scent family ("Warm and Resinous", "Woody and Spicy").

*Removed from the watch list: Clae, Ancestral Cosmetics, Esca and Hearth & Homestead. They came from low-quality roundups and I couldn't verify their sites or catalogs. Vintage Tradition is worth adding later; its site blocked my check today.*

## Patterns that work (borrow the pattern, not the content)
1. **Limited-edition holiday scent in a hero product.** Toups' *Mistletoe* balm, Native's *Candy Cane*. → You already have the Holiday Collection; name the scent notes loudly.
2. **Problem → kit framing.** FATCO: "winter skin is dry + irritated → Winter Essentials Gift Set." → "Winter hands at the sink → Holiday Hand Soap + body butter."
3. **First-timer starter sets.** Toups "Non Toxic Starter Set", Native "Starter Set" (published Oct 16). → Your Clean Start Bundle ($48) and Starter Kit ($60) answer "where do I start?"
4. **Scent discovery.** Native's scent quiz, Nécessaire's scent families. → A "Which holiday scent are you?" carousel.
5. **Stocking stuffers at an impulse price.** Toups $6 lip balm. → Holiday Tinted Lip Balm $12, Holiday Deodorant $18, Lip Oil $20.
6. **Ritual + gift packaging (Nécessaire).** → The Jet Set's "Soil to Skin" pouch and the Holiday Home Collection already look like this. Show the unboxing.
7. **Category-wide on social:** farm/kitchen-rendering origin stories, "tallow vs. expensive moisturizer" comparisons and expert takes drive comments (NPR, PureWow coverage of the trend).

## What customers complain about (your content can answer these)
| Gripe (category-wide) | Your answer post |
|---|---|
| **"Tallow smells like beef"** (very common in tallow balm reviews) | "Does tallow smell like beef? Here's what ours smells like": essential-oil scents (Antioxidant Balm: frankincense, lavender, helichrysum) |
| **Price** (Native's #1 complaint; your customers' too) | Cost per use / how long a jar lasts; bundles; "buy once, gift twice" |
| **Breakouts / "will tallow clog my pores?"** | Honest "who it's for" content: skin-type routines (you sell Foundation/Routine/Ritual by skin type) and patch-test advice. No medical claims. |
| **Natural deodorant adjustment** | "Week 1 vs week 3" expectations post; Baking Soda Free line for sensitive skin |
| **Sticky/tacky body butter** (seen in PP reviews) | Whipped Body Butter how-to: a little, on damp skin, after the shower |

## Gaps to own
1. **The non-toxic holiday home.** No tallow competitor sells candles, room spray and hand soap. Own "a cozy, non-toxic home for the holidays".
2. **Scent as ritual** (proven by your cider reel). Competitors list scent notes; nobody turns them into a seasonal ritual (recipes, a holiday table, a bath before bed).
3. **Real farm origin.** Your founder rendered tallow on her family's farm. That's authentic where many brands are new TikTok entrants. A calm, editorial take beats the hype videos.
4. **Proof at premium.** 5,800+ reviews on the Antioxidant Balm alone. Use the proof instead of discounting early like Native.
5. **"Gifts for the label-reader."** A gift guide built around the person on your list who reads ingredient labels.
