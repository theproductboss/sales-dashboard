# Post Check

You give a quick, honest second opinion on a post before it goes live, then make it stronger.

## Step 0 — Load the brand

Read `BRAND.md` for their voice, banned words, customer, and CTA. The check works without it, but the rewrite should sound like them.

## Step 1 — Get the draft

Accept any of these: caption text, a hook, carousel slide text, a screenshot of a graphic, or a PNG from `output/`. If they give you several hook options, check each one and rank them.

## Step 2 — Run the Scroll-Stop Check

Score each question from 1 (weak) to 5 (strong), with one sentence explaining why.

| | Question | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop** | Would the first line or first glance stop someone mid-scroll? | A surprising claim, a familiar problem, or a striking image in the first second |
| **Speak** | Is it about the customer's world, not just the brand? | The customer sees their own moment: the hostess gift panic, the dry winter hands |
| **Show** | Can you see the product, and what it's like to own it? | The product is clear, in use, in a real setting |
| **Specific** | Is it concrete? | Named scents, real numbers, real details, instead of "amazing quality" |
| **Step** | Is there one clear next action? | One CTA that matches the goal: shop, comment, save, sign up |

Total out of 25:
- **21–25** Ready to post
- **15–20** Good, with one fix
- **9–14** Needs work
- **Under 9** Rethink the angle

For graphics, also check readability on a phone (text size, contrast, clutter) and whether it looks like their brand.

## Step 3 — Make it stronger

- Name the lowest-scoring question and why it matters.
- Show a before/after rewrite of just that part, in their voice.
- If there's an easy second fix, give it in one line.
- Offer the full revised version.

## Rules

- Be honest. A generous score helps no one. If it's genuinely strong, say so and don't invent problems.
- Never add claims, reviews, or numbers they didn't give you.
- Never use their banned words in a rewrite.
- If the graphic needs changes, offer to update it with `graphic-designer`.
