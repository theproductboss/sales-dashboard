Booked = every call on the calendar for the period, by call date. Completed = booked − no-shows − lead cancels − rescheduled. Show rate = completed ÷ (booked − rescheduled). Offer rate = offers ÷ completed. Close rate = wins ÷ completed; the “of booked” line is wins ÷ booked, which is what the spreadsheet called “close rate (% of booked calls)”.

Weeks inside a month follow the spreadsheet: week 1 runs from the 1st through the first Sunday, then Monday–Sunday. A row in the Closer Tracker becomes a call as soon as it has a lead name; leave the date blank and it takes today (or the first day of that week). A nurturing lead counts as overdue when its follow-up date has passed, or when it has no follow-up date and nothing has been changed on it for more than 7 days. Historical rows were imported from the September, October and November trackers; 21 of them had no call date in the sheet and were placed on the first day of their sheet week.
